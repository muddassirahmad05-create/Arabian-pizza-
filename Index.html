Our Story

Where Arabian Flavours Meet Modern Fast Food

Arabian Chick'N is Peshawar's most-loved fast food and pizza restaurant. Since our founding, we've served thousands of families with golden crispy chicken, hand-stretched pizzas and bold, flavourful burgers — all crafted with 100% Halal ingredients.

With three branches across Peshawar and free home delivery, quality food is always within reach. Our tagline says it all: We Serve Quality Plus.
